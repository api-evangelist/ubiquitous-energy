## What the company makes

- UE Power transparent solar coating: small-molecule organic semiconductor dyes absorb ultraviolet
  and infrared light and convert it to electricity while transmitting visible light, so a coated
  window is visually indistinguishable from passive glass and keeps low-e thermal performance.
- Stated performance claims on the company's own site: offsets up to 30% of building electricity;
  non-hazardous, earth-abundant materials; standard industry coating practices; industry-standard
  color neutrality.
- Services sold: technical consultation, R&D, design and engineering for glass and glass-coating
  manufacturers — a licensing and engineering practice, not a software product.
